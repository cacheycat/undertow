# Funding and crowding

## Funding as pressure
Funding is what longs pay shorts (or the reverse) to keep the perp pinned to spot. Persistently positive funding above roughly 15% annualised means longs are paying heavily to stay in: the trade is crowded and fragile to squeezes. Deeply negative funding after a sell-off often marks capitulation shorts, which fuels sharp rebounds. Funding near zero carries no signal.

## Crowding resolves violently
When whale tilt, funding and open interest all point the same way, the exit is narrow: a small adverse move forces liquidations that feed the move. The asymmetric bet is against the crowd when crowding is extreme *and* price has stopped going the crowd's way. Fading a crowd that is still being paid, in a trending tape, is how contrarians get carried out.

## Open interest divergence
Rising price with rising OI is new money trending; rising price with falling OI is shorts covering, which exhausts itself. Falling price with rising OI means shorts pressing, and the fuel for a squeeze builds.
