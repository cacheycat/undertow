# Liquidation mechanics and whale reading

## Liquidation clusters as magnets
Clustered liquidation prices act like magnets in thin tape: once price trades into the cluster, forced closes accelerate the move through it, then it often reverses. A long liquidation cluster a few percent below price is downside fuel; a short cluster just above is squeeze fuel. The side with the nearer, larger cluster is the fragile side.

## Reading profitable whales
Accounts selected for size and recent profit are a biased but useful panel. Agreement among them on an uncrowded trade is the strongest signal; agreement on a trade that already pays high funding mostly means they are the crowd. One whale at high leverage is a liquidation candidate, not a signal.

## Leverage as fragility
Average leverage above roughly 10x among the panel means stop distance is thin and noise can cascade. Low leverage with large notional signals conviction that can sit through drawdown.
