# Writing stances

## Flat is a position
Most hours in most markets carry no edge. A flat call inside the noise band scores as correct, so flat with a reason beats a low-conviction lean. Reserve direction for confluence: structure, flows and crowding agreeing, or crowding extreme against a stalled move.

## Invalidation before entry
A stance without a level or observable change that kills it is not a thesis. Good invalidations: a price reclaiming or losing a 72h range boundary, funding normalising, tilt flipping sign, a liquidation cluster clearing without follow-through.

## Conviction sizing
Conviction 5 means structure, crowding and funding all align and the invalidation is close by: rare by construction. Conviction 1-2 marks a lean that new information should easily reverse. Scoring history keeps conviction honest: a desk that is 40% right at conviction 4 should write smaller numbers.
